# Clankdar identity

The mark is a radar-dish glyph in focus blue, generated by the Slopcamera
pipeline and normalized for the brand catalog. The supplied browser and
Apple touch PNGs are checked by the hashes below.

`icon.png` SHA-256: `669d20930e38a9a8c1c75230f31f4e38d91d3d639ae8622089512aaca9fe4615`.
`apple-icon.png` SHA-256: `4d688cd825cec40e7c6ab9a18525deec20ba3d5839bfda0f47359fd63fc326ac`.
`icon-512.png` SHA-256: `23bc8048d830756c4f372b296133f4841cba373e6c5b9ff2ead2f0dd17dfbfbb`.

`icon-512.png` is rendered from `icons/clankdar.svg` by
`bun site/generate-icon-512.ts`, which leaves out the two hairline bars
at the edges of the traced SVG.

Share images are not checked in. The build renders `/og.png` and every
`/og/…` card from the shared `@hraness/web-discovery` social-image template,
using the one site declaration in `social.ts` and the radar-dish mark in
`marks/clankdar.svg`.
