{{CHALLENGE}}

Three calls. One result to keep.

  1. Create a check

    POST /v1/checks returns fresh prompts, a deadline, and a ticket scoped to that check. Pick the puzzle policy that fits your integration.

  2. Submit the answers

    POST /v1/checks/:id/responses scores one response set against exact answers and returns a signed result. Your system supplies the responses.

  3. Use the receipt

    GET /v1/checks/:id returns the same JSON receipt. Download it, link it from your app, or verify its signature and replay its scores.

Small enough to fit your product.

A receipt carries the puzzle version, recorded responses, exact scores, and issuer signature. Another system can inspect the conditions and replay the result.

Start with one request. Add your own schedule, account model, interface, or admission policy when you need one.

The scoring is open to inspection.

Exact answers, reproducible tasks.

Versioned puzzle generators supply the tasks and reference answers. Scores use deterministic rules, with no judge model. Public calibrations expose strengths and failures by family and difficulty; unaided and tool-agent tracks stay separate.

Portable evidence, simple storage.

Each completed check is one signed JSON result in object storage. The receipt includes what a checker needs to regenerate answered puzzles and replay their scores. Keep your own copy; experimental hosting is not a permanent archive guarantee.

A measured result.
Your decision.

A check measures the submitted answers under a named policy and deadline. It does not identify the model, prove autonomy or uniqueness, measure ongoing availability, or grant permission to act. Public puzzles can be solved with code, delegated, or answered with human help.

The hosted API is in private staging. Your app keeps the issue token server-side and supplies its own solver. Results are public; use non-sensitive context and responses.
